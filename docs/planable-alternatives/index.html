Why look for a Planable alternative?

Planable shines at feedback: pixel-perfect post previews, inline comments and multi-level approvals. Beyond collaboration it is light. There is no unified inbox, no content discovery and only basic analytics, so most teams still need a second tool.

1

No social inbox

You cannot reply to comments or DMs from Planable, so engagement happens elsewhere.

2

Basic analytics

Reporting is limited, making it hard to prove ROI to stakeholders or clients.

3

No content discovery

Planable helps you approve content but not find or generate ideas at scale.

4

Per-workspace pricing

Agencies pay for every client workspace, which adds up quickly.

Questions to ask before you switch

  • Who will use it? Count seats now and in 12 months. Per-seat pricing (like Sprout Social or Agorapulse) changes the math quickly.
  • How many brands or clients? Agencies should look for unlimited or cheap workspaces and white-label reporting.
  • Where does your time go? If it is finding and writing content, prioritise content discovery and AI. If it is replying, prioritise the inbox.
  • Who approves content? Make sure external clients can approve without needing a paid seat.
  • Which networks matter? Check support for Threads, Bluesky, TikTok and Google Business Profile if you use them.
