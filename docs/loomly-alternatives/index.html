Why look for a Loomly alternative?

Loomly (part of Sprout Social since 2024) centres on a clean content calendar, post ideas and approval flows. It is easy to learn, but plan caps on users and accounts, limited analytics and no real content discovery engine make teams outgrow it quickly.

1

User and account caps

The Base plan allows only 2 users and 10 accounts, forcing early upgrades as your team grows.

2

Shallow analytics

Reporting covers the basics but lacks competitor benchmarking and white-label client reports on lower tiers.

3

Generic post ideas

Loomly's ideas are based on holidays and trends, not a searchable discovery engine for your niche.

4

Agency costs add up

Premium plans climb past $275/mo once you need more brands and seats.

Questions to ask before you switch

  • Who will use it? Count seats now and in 12 months. Per-seat pricing (like Sprout Social or Agorapulse) changes the math quickly.
  • How many brands or clients? Agencies should look for unlimited or cheap workspaces and white-label reporting.
  • Where does your time go? If it is finding and writing content, prioritise content discovery and AI. If it is replying, prioritise the inbox.
  • Who approves content? Make sure external clients can approve without needing a paid seat.
  • Which networks matter? Check support for Threads, Bluesky, TikTok and Google Business Profile if you use them.
