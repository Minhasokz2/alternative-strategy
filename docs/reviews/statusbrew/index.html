Statusbrew Review

Rule-based engagement and moderation at scale

★★★★★4.5 out of 5
Visit Statusbrew ↗Compare with ContentStudio
AS AltSocial Editorial TeamUpdated October 2026Hands-on tested

Overview

Statusbrew focuses on engagement: rule-based inbox automation, comment moderation (including ad comments) and SLA tracking. Its publisher is capable, but there is no content discovery and pricing climbs with users.

Best for: Brands with heavy comment volume and moderation needs.

Key features

  • Rule engine for inbox
  • Ad comment moderation
  • SLA reports
  • Planner with approvals
  • Custom reports

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Powerful moderation
  • Great support
  • Ad comment management

Cons

  • No content discovery
  • Pricing rises with seats
  • Learning curve for rules
