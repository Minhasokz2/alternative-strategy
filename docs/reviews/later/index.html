Later Review

Visual-first planning for Instagram, TikTok and creators

★★★★★4.2 out of 5
Visit Later ↗Compare with ContentStudio
AS AltSocial Editorial TeamUpdated October 2026Hands-on tested

Overview

Later built its reputation on a drag-and-drop visual planner for Instagram and its Linkin.bio product. It is great for visual brands, but post limits per plan, a basic inbox and thin reporting for multi-client work limit it for agencies.

Best for: Instagram- and TikTok-led brands and influencer marketers.

Key features

  • Visual Instagram grid planner
  • Linkin.bio
  • Media library with UGC
  • Best time to post
  • Influencer marketing (separate product)

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Beautiful visual planner
  • Strong Instagram and TikTok focus
  • Great link-in-bio

Cons

  • Monthly post caps
  • Weak for text-first networks
  • Limited inbox
  • Agency features are thin
